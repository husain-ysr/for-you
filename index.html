FROM MY HEART TO YOURS

To my Bubu,

My Bubu,

Nine years. When I think about it, I realise that what we have is so much more than a number. It is a thousand little moments, the memories only we understand, the smiles, the silly things, the difficult days, and all the times life somehow brought my heart back to you.

You are woven into so many parts of my life. And if these nine years have taught me anything, it is that love is not only about the beautiful days. It is also about choosing each other, trying again, listening a little more, and holding on to the tenderness that made us “us.”

I cannot promise that every day will be perfect. But I can promise to keep learning how to love you better, to celebrate your happiness, to respect your heart, and to keep making memories with you. I hope we never stop finding reasons to laugh together or little ways to make ordinary days feel special.

Thank you for being part of my story for these nine years. Whatever the next chapters bring, I hope we fill them with honesty, warmth, patience, and a love that keeps growing.

Happy 9th anniversary, Bubu. If I could go back and meet you all over again, I would still want the chance to know you, to laugh with you, and to build this story with you.

Forever your Yasir ♥
